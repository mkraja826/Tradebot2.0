# Commission-aware exit preview
Read-only research on remaining vanilla linear position slice. Observed total fill commissions divided by turnover, assumed equally for entry and exit. For entry E and rate r, break-even exit fill long E(1+r)/(1-r), short E(1-r)/(1+r). Net basis points from actual entry to hypothetical exit less entry and exit notional commission. Mark and matching bracket triggers are hypothetical fill scenarios only. Optional adverse half of recent median full spread applied once at exit, not a prediction or actual cost. Funding, slippage, prior reductions and realized cashflows excluded; no leverage multiplier. No tick-rounded order recommendation, execution or AI feedback. Dashboard reuses execution-cost report without extra read. No trading changes.
