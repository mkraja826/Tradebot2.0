# P2 capital-allocation intelligence
Run from a staged checkout: `python3 -m recover_trading.capital_intelligence --symbol XAUTUSD` (or BTCUSD).
Joins historical AI allocation, settlement wallet, product identity and reconciled fills. Estimates reference and actual-filled notional/margin, planned monetary downside/upside at actual fill, budget deviation and fee burden. Does not equate wallet balance to equity, or estimated margin to actual exchange margin. Missing or ambiguous evidence stays unknown. Cohorts use the observed sample median of planned downside relative to wallet balance; they are descriptive analysis only, never live thresholds. No change to sizing, AI prompts, 20x leverage, execution or services. Validate with VM journals and compare forward results before considering optional evidence integration.
