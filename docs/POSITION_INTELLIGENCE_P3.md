# P3 position intelligence research
Run `python3 -m recover_trading.position_intelligence --symbol BTCUSD` or XAUTUSD from the staged checkout. Matches verified reduce/close receipts to unique actual exit fills and reconciles episode cash/commission before attributing realized contribution. Entry fees are allocated pro rata. Missing receipt identities or ambiguous fills remain unlinked. The measured contribution is not a counterfactual management benefit. Descriptive excursion categories flag potential weak entry/giveback and suspect large excursions, but never alter runtime. Validate coverage against real journals; next research step is independent replay with realistic execution assumptions. No changes to live prompts, review frequency, stop/target policy or service.
