# Order lookup scheduling
The live service previously looked up unknown client IDs in Engine.reconcile and again in Lifecycle.refresh. Lifecycle now performs a single unified pass, with exact product/client/side/size matching before recovering unknown receipt. Acknowledged terminal orders remain skipped. Fresh unresolved and active orders are prioritized oldest lookup first, up to 25 per cycle. Unknowns with at least 3 explicit not-found observations spanning at least 120 seconds are checked on a 1-hour schedule, at most 3 historical lookups per cycle. A non-not-found response resets streak. Existing last-200 snapshots seed schedule on first start. This is scheduling only, not classification as rejected, no replay, no trade admission or risk changes. Wallet, positions, fills, brackets and management reconciliation remain every cycle. Timing comparison is observational, not a latency guarantee.
