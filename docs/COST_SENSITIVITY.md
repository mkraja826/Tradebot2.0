# Cost sensitivity research
The reference replay is recalculated at 0, 5, 10, 15, 20 and 30 basis
points round-trip notional costs, without changing signals or fills.
Break-even cost is the positive mean gross return, or null if there is
no positive gross edge. This is descriptive and not a fee quote or a
portfolio simulation. Development and inspected holdout remain separate.
No execution weight changes are authorized by this report.
