# Management executor v1
Explicit --manage-demo flag, requires --execute-demo. Not enabled by default. On new five-minute captured management context, optional paid proposal evaluation and journaled demo-only execution. Maintain has no mutation. Repair uses position bracket creation; update supports the individually verified stop-loss edit only, not take-profit edits or non-atomic two-leg amendment. Reduce/close use reduce-only orders; full manual market close is fixture-tested, not exchange tested. Require fresh position size, entry, creation time, bracket identity, product and current mark compatibility, and durable one-attempt reservation. Unknown outcomes are not replayed; new management mutations remain unavailable until ambiguous attempts are reconciled by a future dedicated workflow. No equity/loss limits or allocation rules introduced. Opt-in mode routes open positions to management rather than simultaneous entry orders. Default trading behaviour and AI billing unchanged. Existing automatic repair remains active.
