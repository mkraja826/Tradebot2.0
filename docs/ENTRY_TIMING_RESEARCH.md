# Entry timing research v1
Compare original delayed-open entry, additional 5m/10m delay, directional
confirmation, and pullback/reclaim on identical Engine 2.0 replay windows.
Confirmations use completed bars only, enter at the next open, and expire
after two observed bars or before the original exit. Preserve the original
exit time and nonoverlap policy. Report both per-entry and per-opportunity
returns, including zero for untriggered entries, so selective entry cannot
hide its participation rate. Rank development results only, requiring 50
entered outcomes. Previously inspected holdout is descriptive, not new
validation. No AI, exchange orders, or live execution changes.
