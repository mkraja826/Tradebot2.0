# Outcome learning journal
Only reconciled single-Recover-entry closed episodes matching a decision review and net accounting are enrolled. Immutable snapshot of entry, outcome, fees, rule co-occurrence and exit evidence. Follow-up 15m/1h/4h windows start at next full 5m candle after close; distinguish prospective, partly elapsed and retrospective enrollment. Exact same demo instrument, no interpolation, gross directional price change from first open to final close; not counterfactual trading profit, no executable fill or causal attribution. Missing candles remain pending until 24 hours after horizon then explicitly missing. No model calls, network, new orders or feedback into strategy. Separate research store, read-only demo data. Enrollment limited by recent 50 episodes and 100 decision rows; historical samples can be absent. No statistical validation or automatic strategy changes.
