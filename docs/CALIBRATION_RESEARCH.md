# Exploratory calibration
Use the completed public-reference replay. Only development events enter
calibration, grouped by strategy, horizon and seven-day cluster. At least
50 outcomes and eight active full weeks are required for an exploratory
bootstrap interval (2,000 deterministic resamples). Positive lower
bound is a research candidate, not proof, a calibrated probability, a
weight multiplier or a trading gate. Multiple testing, dependence and
limited clusters constrain inference. No production or demo weights
change. The previously inspected holdout is descriptive and cannot be
reused as fresh independent validation. Collect new forward data and
evaluate net-cost sensitivity before proposing weights.
