# Execution-cost intelligence
Read-only observed fill turnover and commission for consistent vanilla product identity, separate reconciled closed and attributed totals. Signed commission is taken from broker fill fields; no maker/taker inference from fill_type. One-way rate uses total commission / total observed turnover. Fee erosion ratio only for positive aggregate gross and reconciled net. Funding field coverage only; no signed settlement assumptions or net after funding. Last hour first valid ticker per minute full quoted spread, not historical trade-time spread. Entry quote attribution from existing exact records only; never reconstruct missing contemporaneous quote. Do not add reference deviation or quoted spread to actual fill-based P&L. No order, model or trading changes.
